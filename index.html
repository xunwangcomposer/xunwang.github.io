Biography

Dedicated young composer and music educator with a strong background in Chinese traditional music, composition, and contemporary music studies. Began studying Zhongruan performance at the age of four and has since developed extensive experience in composition, music education, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Xun Wang is currently studying Composition at Manhattan School of Music under Professor Ashkan Behzadi.

Her music explores timbre, silence, memory and contemporary classical language.
